## 👋 Who We Are

🌬️ **Script Wind** — Where ideas catch wind.  <br>We build scalable web & mobile apps, SaaS platforms, and enterprise systems with modern tech, clean architecture, and strong UI/UX. #scriptwind

> “Technology should empower people, not overwhelm them.”

---

## 🚀 What We Do

- **Mobile App Development** – Premium, high-performance iOS and Android apps built for scale.
- **AI & Automation** – Work smarter with custom AI integration and workflow automation.
- **Website Development** – Fast, modern, converting websites maximizing your online presence.
- **Custom Web Application** – Tailored, powerful web applications built for your unique needs.
- **Enterprise Solutions** – Streamline operations with secure, scalable ERP and CRM systems.
- **UX/UI Design** – Stunning, intuitive designs that users love and engage with.
